Support

Email Show the support address and describe what you were doing when it went wrong. Please do not send lyrics or copyrighted charts — the chords and the section names are enough to work out what happened.

My Planning Center services are empty

Chord Loop lists the upcoming services you are personally scheduled for. If your team hasn't been scheduled yet, or someone else was scheduled in your place, the list will be empty even though the service exists. Check that the same account is scheduled in Planning Center Services.

A song has no chords

Chord Loop can only read a chord chart that was actually attached to the song in Planning Center. If the arrangement has lyrics only, or the chart is a scanned image, there are no chords to read. Photograph the chart with Import a chart instead.

A chord came through wrong

When a chart is read from a photograph, anything Chord Loop is unsure of is shown in red rather than guessed. Tap any chord to correct it before you practice. Sharps read from a photograph are the usual culprit.

No sound

Check that the phone's ringer switch is on rather than silent, and press PLAY once — the first press is what allows a phone to make sound at all.

Disconnecting Planning Center

Open Settings in Chord Loop and choose Disconnect, which revokes the access key. You can also remove it yourself from your Planning Center account under Authorized Apps. Either way, Chord Loop keeps nothing: there are no accounts and no server holding your schedule.
